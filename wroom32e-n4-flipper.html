This page validates the Flipper DfuSe container and USB recovery device before installation. It deliberately does not send an unverified raw binary to STM32 flash.

Recovery mode

Enter recovery: hold Left + Back, release Back, keep holding Left until the blue LED appears, then connect USB-C. For a bricked unit, unplug USB and hold Back for 30 seconds before retrying.

Official Flipper firmware recovery is performed with a full .dfu package. qFlipper is the official USB installer; the Flipper mobile app is the supported Android updater.

USB-C notes
